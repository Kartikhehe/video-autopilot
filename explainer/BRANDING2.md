# Channel branding v2 (premium, Gen-Z, no mascots)

Render with `python3 -m explainer.brand2`. Files go to `out/branding2/`: `<n>_<slug>_banner.png` (2560x1440, logo + text
inside YouTube's 1546x423 safe area so they show on phone, desktop and TV), `<n>_<slug>_avatar.png` (800x800, reads as a
48 px circle) and `sheet.jpg`, which compares them.

**Look**: near-black canvas, aurora gradient light, film grain, a faint grid, glossy 3D orbs, topic chips, a heavy grotesk
wordmark with an *italic serif* accent word, and one minimal glowing logo mark. Fonts are all free (OFL): Unbounded,
Syne, Bricolage Grotesque, Instrument Serif, Space Grotesk.

Checked Nov 2026: a web search found no YouTube channel already using these four names. Grab the handle before
announcing the name.

**Why Mind Glitch**: it says exactly what the channel covers (bhang, porn, dopamine, sleep, caffeine: things that
glitch your brain). It's short and global, works as a verb ("that's a mind glitch"), and the glitch logo is the most
recognisable avatar at small sizes. Pick **Dimaag Kharab** if the channel is Hindi/Hinglish-first for Indian Gen-Z.
