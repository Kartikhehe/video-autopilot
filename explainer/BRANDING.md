# Channel branding options

Render all of them with `python3 -m explainer.brand`. The files go to `out/branding/`:
`option<N>_<slug>_banner.png` (2560x1440, with all text inside YouTube's 1546x423 mobile-safe area),
`option<N>_<slug>_avatar.png` (800x800, built to read as a small circle) and `branding_sheet.jpg`, which compares the options.
Every name, mascot and piece of art is original. A quick web search found no big channel using any of these names
(Nov 2026). Check the handle is free before you register it.

The channel makes animated, narrated explainers about the brain, the body, and the strange things people eat,
drink and consume. They are colourful and science-backed, and every number on screen is sourced.

---

## Option 1: Neon Neuron
- **Handle:** `@NeonNeuron` (fallback `@NeonNeuronExplains`)
- **Tagline:** Your brain, explained loudly.
- **Mascot:** a glowing pink neuron with one big curious eye and a yellow-tipped axon "tail".
- **Description:** Neon Neuron turns the weirdest questions about your brain and body into bright, animated
  stories. What does bhang, caffeine or a sleepless night actually do inside you? Every video is narrated, research-backed
  and made to be fun.
- **Palette:** night `#1a0b4a` · violet `#4b1590` · magenta `#c21f7a` · neon pink `#ff4fa3` · lilac `#7b5cff` ·
  cyan `#2bd1ff` · signal yellow `#ffd23f`

## Option 2: Andar Ki Baat (अंदर की बात)
- **Handle:** `@AndarKiBaat` (fallback `@AndarKiBaatScience`)
- **Tagline:** What's really happening inside you.
- **Mascot:** a cheerful brain buddy with little arms and yellow shoes, waving.
- **Description:** Andar Ki Baat means "the inside story". We animate what really happens inside your body
  and mind, from festival bhang to late-night scrolling. The explainers are short, colourful and science-backed, and the
  channel has a desi heart.
- **Palette:** plum `#3b1070` · rose `#d1366b` · saffron `#ffb02e` · marigold `#ffd23f` · brain pink `#ff7eb6` ·
  lilac `#7b5cff`

## Option 4: Tiny Big Questions

- **Description:** Tiny Big Questions asks the small questions with surprisingly big answers. Why does bhang hit

---

**Recommendation: Option 1, Neon Neuron.** It is the strongest brand. The name is short, memorable and works
worldwide. It fits the vivid neon look of the videos. The one-eyed mascot is the most distinctive avatar at comment
size and can appear as a recurring character in videos. Option 2 is the best choice if the channel is aimed mainly at
Indian, Hindi-speaking viewers.
